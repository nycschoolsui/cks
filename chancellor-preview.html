The Chancellor’s Priorities for New York City Public Schools

The mission of New York City Public Schools is to ensure that every child grows into a confident, capable, and compassionate young adult, prepared to thrive in their community and the wider world. We are committed to sustaining a rich, vibrant constellation of schools across our system where every child feels safe, supported, and engaged in rigorous learning.

Schools Chancellor Kamar H. Samuels leads the nation’s largest school system with a clear set of priorities that guide decisions across policy, practice, and community engagement. Those three priorities are safety, academic rigor, and true integration.

Chancellor's Values

Safety

For the Chancellor, safety extends beyond physical protection to whether students feel socially, emotionally, and culturally affirmed, and have agency to create change and lead in their school communities.

Academic Rigor

Supportive and Affirming Environments Where Students Feel Like They Belong

To learn and grow, students need to feel physically safe and believe that they are part of a community of peers and adults who welcome them. For the Chancellor, safety means more than physical protection. It also means that students feel supported socially, emotionally, and culturally, and that they have a real voice in shaping and leading their school community.

When students feel safe, they are more willing to engage, participate, and take the academic risks that will enable them to thrive.

Safe schools are places where:
  • Students are invited to inform and improve the school experience, and provided opportunities to lead and create solutions to problems.
  • Students feel a sense of belonging and know they are respected for who they are.
  • Caring adults build trusting relationships and provide consistent support.
  • Students’ social, emotional, and mental well-being are actively supported.
  • Families are valued partners and trust the school to understand and respond to their children’s n eeds.
Providing Challenging,  Evidence-Based Instruction, and Meaningful Opportunities for Every Student

Every student deserves access to challenging instruction that includes core academic skills, real-world learning that prepares students for future opportunities, and civic participation in a diverse, complex society.

Academic rigor looks like:
  • Setting and maintaining developmentally appropriate academic achievement expectations.
  • Strong, grade-level instruction grounded in evidence-based practices, including those used in NYC Reads and NYC Solves.
  • Enrichment opportunities that expand students’ access to high-wage, high-demand careers, such as through our Student Pathways programs
  • Programs that foster a global mindset and critical thinking skills, like International Baccalaureate.
  • Inclusive learning experiences that support students with disabilities and multilingual learners, like Dual Language classrooms, Nest, AIMS, and Horizon programs, and more. 
  • Providing multicultural and interdisciplinary learning experiences through resources like Hidden Voices, Respect for All, and Civics for All.
  • Preparing students for civic participation by supporting respectful discussion, empathy, and the ability to engage thoughtfully with different viewpoints, while ensuring educators are equipped to navigate discussion of complex current events.
Developing Leaders Who Can Connect Across Differences and Build a Brighter Future

True integration is the Chancellor’s defining value. It is the through-line that shapes how safety and academic rigor are sustained across the system. When a school is truly integrated, the student body reflects the full diversity of New York City, and students have meaningful opportunities to understand their own identities and learn from those of their peers.

To realize true integration across our system, each community’s context, history, and needs will shape how integration strategies are developed and implemented in partnership with families, educators, and students.

True integration looks like:
  • Creating school communities that reflect the full diversity of New York City.
  • Building robustly enrolled schools that sustain strong programs, enrichment, and advanced coursework.
  • Ensuring educators have the training and support they need to implement culturally responsive and sustaining education (CRSE), strengthening representation, equitable resources, and relationships across school communities.
  • Expanding access to high-demand school options for students and families across the city.
  • Reducing concentrated vulnerability so that students are not isolated by poverty, disability, housing instability, or language needs. This is why the Chancellor frames integration as a value. When schools are truly integrated, they create the conditions that make both rigorous learning and affirming safety possible, especially for students who have historically had the least access to opportunity.

Our Shared Commitment and Goal

Safety, academic rigor, and true integration together form the framework that guides how New York City Public Schools advances its mission in collaboration with students, families, educators, staff, and community partners. As the Chancellor continues to engage communities across the city, their lived experiences and perspectives will help shape how these priorities are carried out in practice.

Under Chancellor Samuels’ leadership, we are committed to building schools that embody these priorities for every child in every neighborhood.
