Our City.
Our Future.

During my first 100 days as Chancellor, I listened closely as students, families, and educators shared their questions, concerns, and ideas.

I met with thousands of people in schools across the five boroughs.

What We Heard

People love their public schools and want the world to see the great work happening inside them. 

Safety Starts with Being Seen

Students feel safe when connected to a community of peers and adults.

Challenge and Inspire Every Student

Students want to feel challenged and supported. They want access to exciting curriculum and meaningful real-world experiences. 

Partnership Matters.

Families want clear communication and real influence in shaping the vision of our schools.

High Quality Schools for Everyone

Quality education shouldn't depend on your address. Integration means bilingual and specialized programs, culturally responsive curricula, diverse staff, and high expectations for all students.  

Your Input Shaped My Vision for NYCPS.

Our schools are places where young people learn how to be confident and empathetic learners and gain the skills needed for social mobility in a polarized society. 

The Road Ahead.

Your voices have given us a clear roadmap. Let's keep working together, hand in hand, to lift up our children.

Our Shared Commitment and Goal

Safety, academic rigor, and true integration together form a unified framework for how New York City Public Schools will advance our mission. These values will be advanced in collaboration with students, families, educators, staff, and community partners. As the Chancellor continues to engage communities across the city, their lived experiences and perspectives will help shape how these priorities are carried out in practice.

Under Chancellor Samuels’ leadership, we are committed to building schools that embody these priorities—for every child in every neighborhood.
